01 // ABOUT US

Local, High-Trust Biophysical Inference

AETHER is built to bypass the expensive cloud bills and massive compute limitations historically associated with computational biology [1]. Operating entirely as a CPU-only enzyme mutation discovery engine, it processes complex protein structures on consumer-grade hardware in under 69 seconds [1].

The platform's capability is anchored in rigorous molecular verification [1]. AETHER independently discovered the highly effective W159H mutation, which stabilizes the pocket conformation of PET-degrading enzymes, proving that search-space optimization can be executed successfully without black-box cloud dependencies [1].
